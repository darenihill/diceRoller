# User stories, walked through the app (2026-11-09)

Walked in Playwright (Chromium, 390×844, touch, scale 1) against a
production build of the release branch after the words-on-screen pass.
Google Analytics only loads away from localhost, so the metrics stories were
walked at the machine's network address. Screenshots are in Daren's project
files under `dice-roller/2026-11-09/review42/`, one per finding.

| # | Story | Test script | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | First visit: roll and read the total | Open the site, tap Roll | Works. The total shows on the Roll button about 2 s after the tap (`s1-first-roll.png`) |
| 2 | Put my child's photo on a die, then take the die away | Die › Settings › Custom faces, add "Mia" and "Leo", choose a picture for Mia, Done, Remove the die, wait out Undo, reload | **Kept after removal, idea.** The photo stays in the phone's storage after the die is gone and the page reloads. It is only let go the next time any picture is added (`s2-picture-face.png`, `s2-removed-die.png`) |
| 3 | Turn page metrics off | Menu › Customize, scroll to the bottom, switch off, reload | **Not fully off, fixed.** The switch stopped the counts, but every visit still downloaded Google's tag script, and Google's two ID cookies (kept until December 2027) stayed. Now, with metrics off, nothing is fetched from Google's tag server and both cookies are deleted; switching back on starts again without a reload (`s3-metrics-switch.png`, `s3-metrics-off-after.png`) |
| 4 | New phone: metrics off, then restore my backup from the old phone | Old phone: Save, Games › Export backup. New phone: metrics off, Games › Import backup, Customize | **Undone without asking, fixed.** The old backup said "on" (the default), and Import quietly turned metrics back on. A backup can now turn metrics off, never on (`p1-import-turns-metrics-on-before.png`, `p1-import-turns-metrics-on-after.png`) |
| 5 | See what the app keeps about me, and wipe it | Roll ×3, Menu › Usage stats, then History › Clear History | **No way to wipe, idea.** Usage stats lists first and last use, sessions and rolls; its only way out is "Clearing your browser data", and Clear History leaves the stats (`s4-usage-stats.png`, `s4-history-cleared.png`) |
| 6 | Share a die I named "Rent: Sam owes" | Die › Settings › Name, Menu › Share | Works as designed: the link carries die and game names, not pictures; Help says so for pictures. Nothing new (`s5-share-link.png`) |

## Fixes built

- Story 3: with page metrics off, the site no longer downloads Google's tag
  script (home page and the four search pages), and Google's `_ga` cookies
  are deleted when the switch goes off and on every visit while it stays
  off (`src/test/review20261109.test.tsx`).
- Story 4: Import backup keeps metrics off when the backup says on
  (`src/test/review20261109.test.tsx`).
- Story 11: closing any dialog opened from the menu puts focus on the menu
  button (`src/test/review20261109.test.tsx`).

## Standards applied

- Privacy | Customize, metrics switch | Before: off stopped the counts, but each visit still fetched Google's script and Google's ID cookies stayed for 13 months | After: off fetches nothing from Google's tag server and deletes its cookies (`src/test/review20261109.test.tsx`)
- Privacy | Games, Import backup | Before: a backup made with metrics on turned them back on | After: a backup can turn metrics off, never on (`src/test/review20261109.test.tsx`)
- Accessibility | Every dialog opened from the menu | Before: closing it left keyboard and screen reader users at the start of the page | After: focus goes back to the menu button (`src/test/review20261109.test.tsx`)

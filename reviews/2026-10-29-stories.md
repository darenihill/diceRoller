## Fixes built

- Adding, removing or replacing a die's faces (count, custom faces, Decision Maker, Compass) releases
  its hold, so an edited die rolls again. Recolouring a face keeps a hold.
- Word-only dice (Yes/No, Compass) no longer show a 0 total: the button keeps
  saying Roll.
- Word faces get a little more room per letter, so "YES" and "MAYBE" stay on
  one line.
- Percentile dice read 00 + 0 as 100.
- Preset dice start on a face they can show (D&D d4, Tenzi).

## Standards applied

- Say what happened: Roll with every die held says "All dice are held. Tap a die
  to let it roll again." instead of silently logging a repeat roll.
- Undo, no lost work: loading a game from Games, opening a share link, and the
  default opening over your last dice each offer Undo ("Loaded Catan · Undo",
  "Opened shared dice · Undo", "Opened your default dice · Undo").

## Suggested (Focus tab)

- Share opens your phone's share sheet, with a Copy button as backup.

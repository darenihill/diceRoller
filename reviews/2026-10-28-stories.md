Walked in Playwright (Chromium, 390×844, touch and mobile on, real taps)

### 1. The faces count, a template, or a face's X wipes custom faces with no Undo — (b) standard gap, standard 2 (Undo)
Screenshot: `shots/stories-1.png` (after one tap on +: "No custom faces added", count 7).
The X on a die, Clear All and a deleted save all offer Undo; changes inside
a die's settings never do. One shared handler in App can offer Undo
whenever an edit drops a face's text (colour changes keep the text, so they
don't trigger it).

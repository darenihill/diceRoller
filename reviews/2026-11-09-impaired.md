## Undo windows

- The bar lasts 7 s, pauses while touched and sits just above the bottom
  buttons (y 680, or y 626 in RPG mode above the NORM and ±0 chips). A tired
  player sees it where the thumb already is.
- The gap was the first half second: the bar opens over the button that was
  just tapped. A second tap went to × (Remove in the action row) or to Undo
  (Clear all). **Fixed:** a press in the bar's first 500 ms does nothing.
- A newer action replaces the bar (standard 2). That was fine except when the
  newer action was the same mistake twice. **Fixed for games and dice:** a
  second delete or remove while the bar is up joins it.
- After 7 s, nothing brings a game, a cleared table or cleared History back.
  That is the queued Recently deleted item, not repeated here.

## Things that are easy to get wrong, left as they are

- Two Roll buttons in RPG mode: the small one by the roll box (top, out of
  reach) rolls what you typed, the big one rolls the dice on screen. With a
  roll typed, the thumb hits the big one (`c5-typed-then-big-roll.png`). Idea.
- Dialogs close with × at the top right (y 140 to 306), the hardest corner
  for a right thumb. Back and a tap outside also close them, which most
  people won't think of while tipsy. Idea (bottom sheets).

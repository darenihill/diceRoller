# User stories, walked again (2026-10-26)

Walked in Playwright (Chromium, iPhone 13 at 390 px, real taps; also 360,
320, 260 and 195 px wide) against a production build of master. The stories
of 2026-10-22 to 10-24 still pass; these are the new ones, aimed at the
large-text, screen-reader and keyboard angles this pass asks for.

| # | Story | Before this pass | Now |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cold visitor on a common 360 px Android phone: Roll, read the total. | Works, but Hold All sits 4 px under the add button. | Fixed: the bottom row shrinks below 380 px; no overlap at 360 or 320. |
| 2 | Same visitor with Android display size "Large" (about 320 px): add a die. | **Stuck-ish:** the add button is half under Hold All; a tap on its left half holds every die instead. | Fixed (see 1). |
| 3 | Screen reader user: open a die's settings. | **Stuck:** the die was a button, and a button's inner buttons are hidden from screen readers, so settings, copy, roll-one and remove could not be reached. | Fixed: the die is a named group ("Red showing 3"), with Hold, Settings for Red, Copy Red, Roll only Red and Remove Red. |
| 4 | Screen reader user, two dice: remove the second. | Heard "Remove Die" twice, no way to tell which. | Fixed: "Remove d20". |
| 5 | Keyboard user: open the menu, change mind. | Escape did nothing; Tab past twelve items. | Fixed: Escape closes it and returns focus to the menu button. |
| 6 | RPG player with a screen reader: set +2 and ADV, roll. | Roll read "Roll dice" whatever it showed; ADV/NORM/DIS never said which was picked. | Fixed: "Roll dice +2", then "9 (7+2), roll again"; ADV/NORM/DIS, RPG Mode and Hold All say pressed or not. |
| 7 | Windows high contrast: tell the dice apart. | Dice and buttons lost their fill and edges: bare numbers and loose icons. | Fixed: dice and buttons draw an edge in high contrast. |

| 9 | 200% zoom (195 px wide). | Hold All and Add overlap; the dice shrink to 80 px. | Better at 150% (260 px, no overlap); still overlapping at 200%. GOAL.md Next item. |

## Fixes built

- The bottom row (Hold All, Add, Roll and the menu button) shrinks from
  72 px to 60 px below 380 px wide, and to 52 px (Roll drops its icon)
  below 300 px, so nothing overlaps on small phones or at 150% zoom.
- Each die is a named group with its own Hold button; its buttons name the
  die ("Settings for Red", "Copy Red", "Roll only Red", "Remove Red").
- The Roll button's spoken name matches what it shows.
- Escape closes the menu.
- High contrast mode draws dice and button edges.

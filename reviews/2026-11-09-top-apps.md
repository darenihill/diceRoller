# What top apps do differently, second pass (2026-11-09)

The 6 November pass compared each flow with dice apps and calculators, and
most of its ideas are built (History reuse, History as dice, Save fills in a
name). This pass asks a narrower question for the same flows: how do the best
apps of any kind handle mis-taps, undo and one-handed reach? No project
details went into any search. Claims a source didn't back were left out.

## Not suggested

- Lock-by-value, onboarding, 3D dice, a main-screen Share: turned down or
  skipped before.
- A longer Undo time like Gmail's 30 s: the 7 s is the standard across all
  of Daren's apps, and Recently deleted covers later.

## Ideas (for the Desk)

- With a roll typed, the big Roll button rolls what you typed.
- Dialog buttons at least 48 px tall.
- Dialogs open from the bottom and swipe down to close.

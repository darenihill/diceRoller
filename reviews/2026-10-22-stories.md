## Fixes built

- Save Game and Share Link buttons used a style that does not exist; they now use the filled button (Save was black on dark grey).
- The bin on a saved game is named "Delete <name>" for screen readers.
- Rolling with no dice does nothing, instead of logging a total made only of the modifier (unit test added).

## Standards applied

None built: Dice Roller reaches anyone, so standard gaps (Undo, no lost work, empty states, duplicates) went to the Focus tab as cards.

## Suggested (Focus tab)

Undo after removing; Tapping a die doesn't hold it; Safer saving; Say the total includes the modifier; Empty screen offers next step.

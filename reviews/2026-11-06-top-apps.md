# What top apps do differently (2026-11-06)

Walked master at 390 × 844 (touch, mobile) on the local production build. Followed the comparison-benchmark skill's question (who does each *mechanism* best, in any category) and its privacy rule (no project details went into any search). Its Workflow fan-out, cost ladder and goal queueing are out of scope for one third of a review, so research and the adversarial check were done in this one pass. ## App-standards gaps (fixes, noted separately)

- **Say what happened (standard 4):** after a typed roll, the Undo bar truncates to "Rolled d20+…" at 390 px (`shots/top-type-roll-result.png`). Built in this pull request: the message wraps to two lines.

## Candidate ideas (strongest first)

### 1. Roll a past roll again from History
- **Why:** calculators, Roll20 and Owlbear all treat history as a launcher. Ours is read-only.
- **Today:**
  - History rows can't be tapped.
  - Going back to a to-hit roll after a damage roll means retyping it or rebuilding the dice.
- **Matters:**
  - RPG turns alternate between two or three rolls.
  - It removes retyping without adding a screen.
- **Ways:**
  - **A. Tap a History row.** Trade-off: one tap reaches every roll, but it replaces the dice on screen.
    - Tapping a row brings back those dice (and the typed roll, the +2 and ADV) and rolls them.
    - The bottom Undo bar says "Rolled 2d6+3 again · Undo" and puts back the dice that were out.
  - **B. Recent rolls under the type box (RPG Mode).** Trade-off: no menu trip, but only for typed rolls.
    - The last three typed rolls show as small chips under the box. A tap rolls one.
    - Chips are different from "Save this roll" buttons: they come and go on their own.
- **Before:** History is a list to read.
- **After:** History is a list to read, and each row rolls again with one tap.
- **Kind:** feature · **Effort:** M · **Spread:** a few screens · **Impact:** high
- **Sources:** [Roll20 wiki](https://wiki.roll20.net/Dice_GUI), [Roll20 help](https://help.roll20.net/hc/articles/360039674933), [Owlbear Rodeo](https://blog.owlbear.rodeo/owlbear-rodeo-2-0-dice-deep-dive/), [iPhone Life on Calculator](https://iphonelife.com/content/iphone-calculator-history)
- **Screenshot:** `shots/top-history.png`

### 2. History rows show the dice, not "d6 #1: 2"
- **Why:** Foundry and our own Table view show each die. History uses a third, wordier way.
- **Today:**
  - Rows read "d6 #1: 6, d6 #2: 1 · Total: 7".
  - Dropped (ADV/DIS) and held dice look the same as the rest.
- **Matters:**
  - Built once (standard 3): the Table view's small die tiles already exist.
  - Tiles can show the die colour and its name, which suits a colorblind reader better than a colour alone.
- **Happens:**
  - Each row shows the Table view's tiles (dropped ones struck through) and the total on the right.
  - A tap on the tiles (or on "Roll again" if idea 1 takes the row tap) opens the full math.
- **Before:** "d6 #1: 2, d6 #2: 4 · Total: 6"
- **After:** `[2] [4]  = 6`, in the same tiles as Table view.
- **Kind:** look · **Effort:** S–M · **Spread:** one screen · **Impact:** med
- **Sources:** [Foundry dice](https://foundryvtt.com/article/dice/), [TidBITS on PCalc](https://tidbits.com/2019/07/17/take-a-chance-with-dice-by-pcalc/)
- **Screenshot:** `shots/top-history.png`

### 3. Say the total out loud (a Customize switch)
- **Why:** Pixels and PCalc, the two most specialised dice apps, both speak results. It reaches the whole table without passing the phone.
- **Today:**
  - The result is visual only (and a buzz). Table view needs the phone turned toward people.
- **Matters:**
  - Everyone at the table hears "Eighteen" at once, including across a big table or a dim room.
  - The browser has the speech built in, so nothing new is downloaded.
- **Happens:**
  - Customize gets a "Say the total" switch, off by default and kept on this device.
  - When the dice land, the phone says the total ("eighteen"), or "natural twenty" on a d20 20.
  - It follows the sound setting: silent mode stays silent.
- **Before:** you read the total and call it out.
- **After:** the phone calls it out.
- **Kind:** feature · **Effort:** S · **Spread:** one screen (plus the landing moment) · **Impact:** med
- **Sources:** [Pixels on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1532993928), [pcalc.com/dice](https://pcalc.com/dice)
- **Screenshot:** `shots/top-table-4dice.png`

### 4. Save suggests a name
- **Why:** Apple Notes never asks you to name things. The first line becomes the title.
- **Today:**
  - "Enter a name for this game:" opens empty.
- **Matters:**
  - On a phone mid-game, typing a name is the slowest part of saving.
- **Happens:**
  - The box opens filled and selected: the loaded game's name ("Catan") or the dice ("2 d6 + d20").
  - Typing replaces it, and Save keeps it as it is.
- **Before:** an empty box.
- **After:** "2 d6" ready, so Save is one tap.
- **Kind:** wording · **Effort:** S · **Spread:** one screen · **Impact:** low–med
- **Sources:** [Apple Notes help](https://help.apple.com/notes/mac/10.12/en.lproj/not9474646a9.html)
- **Screenshot:** `shots/top-save.png`

Deliberately not suggested: a rolls-left counter (the same family as the skipped round counter), a Share button on the main screen (skipped), lock-by-value commands, onboarding, and 3D tumbling dice (pull request 75).

## Small design tweaks (≥60% sure, no behaviour change)

1. **Name the picked colour in words:** "Die color · White" beside the dots, and the same under Custom faces' Color. WCAG 1.4.1. Daren is colorblind. `shots/top-die-settings.png`
2. **A small "Total" caption** over the number on the Roll button after a roll, as Google does. `shots/top-roll-total.png`
3. **Share wording:** "Copy this link to share your dice configuration:" → "Anyone with this link gets these dice:". `shots/top-share.png`
4. **Undo bar after a typed roll:** say "Rolled d20 adv +5" in full, wrapping to two lines, instead of "Rolled d20+…". `shots/top-type-roll-result.png`
5. **History wording (if idea 2 isn't built):** "2 + 4 = 6" to match Table view's math, instead of "d6 #1: 2, d6 #2: 4 · Total: 6".
6. **Preset spelling:** "Yatzee" → "Yatzy", the generic name ([YATZY with Buddies](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1127362559)). It's correctly spelled and avoids the trademark. `shots/top-games.png`

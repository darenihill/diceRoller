# Combined (2026-11-10): stories, security, privacy

- **The fix that hands focus back can press a button.** On 9 November focus
  was sent back to the menu button after a dialog, for keyboard and screen
  reader users. The stories walk then pressed Enter in Save and the menu
  opened: the Enter that saved also pressed the button that took focus. A
  keyboard lens alone sees focus land in the right place; a stories walk
  alone sees "the menu opened" with no reason. Fixed in Save, the one dialog
  that closes on Enter.
- **The Undo bar was placed for the main screen only.** The impaired lens put
  it right above the Roll buttons, where the thumb is. Dialogs sit in the
  middle of the screen with their own Done at the bottom, the same height.
  Every remove inside a dialog (a face, a picture, a game, History) opened the
  bar on that Done, so the "finish" tap became Undo. Fixed: with a dialog
  open, the bar sits under it.
- **One rule makes names safe for both people and checks.** Security found a
  hidden character could sneak "Catan" past the name check; the stories walk
  found title-case tab names overflow at a larger text size. Both are about
  what a name looks like on screen, not what its characters are. The check
  now compares names as they read.
- **Privacy's open gaps are all "delete doesn't reach every copy".** History
  keeps a deleted game's name, the offline cache keeps ad ids, and old
  pictures linger. The queued Recently deleted and the Erase everything idea
  already cover them.

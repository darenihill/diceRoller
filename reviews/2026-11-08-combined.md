# Combined (2026-11-08): stories, security, inversion

- **A link that does nothing, from three sides.** The walk got stuck when a
  friend's link was pasted into the open tab (stories 6). Security saw the
  same gap as a spoofing risk: someone thinks they loaded the friend's dice
  and rolls their own. Inversion lists "a link that does nothing sometimes"
  as a top way to frustrate. One change answers all three, and is built in
  this pull request.
- **Every way in replaces your dice.** Link, game, History row, typed roll,
  default dice and Clear all each swap the dice out with only a 7-second
  Undo (stories 3, 4; inversion 1, 8). The queued Recently deleted item is
  the net for all of them; Clear all is added to it today. Building it is
  the single change that answers the most from this pass.
- **Things you can't see.** Die actions appear only after a tap (stories 8,
  inversion 4) and Google Analytics is on without a word on the first screen
  (security, inversion 5). Both are "tell me once on the first visit". They
  are separate ideas on the Focus tab, but could share one first-visit line.
- **The queue is the pain.** Four inversion rows are fixes already queued
  since 6 and 7 November. Next goal: build Recently deleted and the three
  small queued fixes (Messages show over Undo, Same-name typed roll asks,
  Loading text) before new features.

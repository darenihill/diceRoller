## Fixes built

- Story 1: Custom Faces shows one face card per row until two really fit, so
  nothing spills out of the panel (phone and computer).
- Story 2: Color, Icons and Text drop to two a row when the words need it.
- Story 3: phone menu tiles drop to three a row at a large text size, so no
  word splits.

All three are pinned in `src/test/review20261111.test.ts` and were measured
again in the browser after the build (rows 256px wide for a 190px row; menu
tiles 114px for an 86px word).

## Standards applied

- Accessibility | Phone menu | Before: at 200% text "Customize" and "Feedback" split across two lines | After: three tiles a row at that size, every word whole (`src/test/review20261111.test.ts`)
- Accessibility | Custom Faces | Before: at 200% text Color, Icons and Text ran past their borders | After: two a row at that size (`src/test/review20261111.test.ts`)

## Suggested next goals

Dice Roller is in Maintain (fixes and Daren's own asks only), so these are
listed here and not sent to the Desk.

- **Erase everything says settings and default dice go too.** Why: the
  question names games, rolls, pictures and stats, but the switch also
  resets default dice, die sizes and the theme. Cues: wording, one screen,
  effort S, impact low, confidence 55.
- **At large text the phone menu keeps Saves on the first screen.** Why: at
  200% Games, the most used tile, is now one scroll down. Cues: layout, one
  screen, effort S, impact low, confidence 40.

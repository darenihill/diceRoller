Privacy

Stays on this phone

Your dice, games, History and pictures. Nothing you type, name or photograph is ever sent. Erase everything, at the bottom of Usage stats, takes it all off this phone.

Goes to Google while Page metrics is on

Your visits and taps, with each roll’s total and RPG settings; how many dice you roll, save, share or add; whether a roll was typed (never the words); the popular game you load, the theme you pick and the switches you turn on or off; how far you scroll; the page you came from and your screen size; your rough city, device, browser and language; a random ID Google keeps on this phone for up to 2 years after your last visit.

Turning it off

Customize, Page metrics, Off. Then nothing more goes to Google and its ID is deleted from this phone.

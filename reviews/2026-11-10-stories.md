# User stories, walked through the app (2026-11-10)

Walked in Playwright (Chromium, 390×844, touch, scale 1) against a production
build of the release branch (`npx vite build && npx vite preview`), after the
trim pass, the phone fixes (no menu shapes, "Die Settings" and "Custom Faces"
tabs, the Release chip fading in) and the 9 November Undo fixes. This pass
looked at what changed since then and at steps the earlier walks hadn't
tried: the keyboard's Enter in Save, finishing a dialog right after a
remove, and a larger text size. Screenshots are in Daren's project files
under `dice-roller/2026-11-10/review48/`.

## Fixes built

- Story 1: Enter in Save saves and nothing more; the menu stays shut.
- Stories 2–4: with a dialog open, the Undo bar and messages sit at the
  screen's bottom edge, under the dialog, never over its Done.
- Story 5: the die settings tabs wrap at a large text size instead of
  overlapping.

All three are tested in `src/test/review20261110.test.tsx` and were walked
again in the browser after the build.

## Standards applied

- Text input and the keyboard | Save | Before: Enter in the name box saved, then opened the menu | After: Enter saves and the menu stays shut (`src/test/review20261110.test.tsx`)
- Undo | Die settings, Games, History | Before: the Undo bar opened on top of the dialog's Done and Copy, so Done after a remove pressed Undo | After: with a dialog open the bar sits under it, at the screen's bottom edge (`src/test/review20261110.test.tsx`)
- Accessibility | Die settings tabs | Before: at 200% text size "Die Settings" and "Custom Faces" ran into each other and under × | After: each wraps onto two lines (`src/test/review20261110.test.tsx`)

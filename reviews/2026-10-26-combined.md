# Across the four questions (2026-10-26)

1. **The ring of buttons around a die is the weak spot from three sides.**
   Screen readers couldn't reach it (fixed today), it has no words on a
   phone (heuristic 6), and with six dice it shrinks to 30 px (the Desk card
   Daren is discussing: "maybe below a certain size the options pop out").
   One answer covers all three: when dice are small, a tap opens a small
   sheet with labelled buttons (Hold, Roll only this, Copy, Settings,
   Remove) instead of the ring. That is his own suggestion on that card, so
   it is noted there rather than filed as a new idea.
2. **Narrow screens and large text are the same users.** The overlap fixed
   today hit 360 px phones, Android's Large display size and browser zoom
   alike; the px-only fonts mean zoom is their only tool. Text that follows
   the phone's setting (Focus card) and the 200% zoom layout (GOAL.md) are
   the two halves left.
3. **Fast rollers wait twice:** 0.8 s per roll on a phone (speed) and a Tab
   hunt for Roll on a computer (heuristic 7). The two Focus cards, "tap Roll
   again to land the dice" and "Space or R to roll", are small and
   independent.

# Plain and consistent words (2026-11-06)

Every label, button and message read against the US Federal Plain Language
Guidelines, GOV.UK style and Material 3 writing. Full audit (44 rewrites,
marked safe or check) is in the project files,
reviews/dice-roller-2026-11-06-second/copy-audit.md.

## Biggest findings

1. Saved dice go by five names: game, save, set, preset, configuration ("Games"
   in the menu under "Saves", a dialog "Games & Saves" with "Your Saves" and
   "Presets", Help's "predefined configurations"). The app mostly says game.
2. Two landing-page lines sent people to menu items that don't exist ("under
   Load", "the D&D set from the menu").
3. One die's options are called "Dice Settings"; "Settings" also names a menu
   group, while the app's own options are "Customize".
4. Backup and share messages used "successfully", "Failed to", "parse",
   "Invalid … structure" and exclamation marks.
5. Help is the weakest copy: "Simply click/tap", "chip", "polyhedral", items of
   52 to 75 words.
6. Title Case is mixed with sentence case, sometimes side by side in the menu
   ("Usage Stats" next to "Table view").
7. Typed-roll notes said "sides" where Die settings say "faces".

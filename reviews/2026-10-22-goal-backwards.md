# Goal check: working backwards (2026-10-22)

Question: write the launch post for Dice Roller a few months from now in five
sentences. Does it describe the current goal, or a different one?

## The launch post (early 2027)

> **Dice Roller: the dice your game actually needs, free in your browser.**
> Open customdiceroller.com and roll in one tap, or load Catan, D&D, Zombie
> Dice and nine more games with their dice already set up. Make dice that
> don't exist anywhere else: your own words, colours and pictures on any
> face, symbols that score points, any number of sides. Mistakes are easy to
> take back, your saved games come first, and a phone's back button behaves
> the way you expect. There's no account, no ads and nothing to install;
> send a friend one link and they have your exact dice.

## Does it describe the current goal?

**Yes, with two things the goal line leaves unsaid.**

| Sentence | Goal line covers it? | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1-2: roll fast, games preloaded | yes ("board games and tabletop RPGs") | presets, landing pages |
| 3: dice nobody else makes | yes ("customizable") | pictures, symbols, colours, built Oct 12-17 |

The fifth sentence is the app's strongest promise to users and its main
difference from phone dice apps, yet it lives only in ROADMAP.md. It is
also exactly what is in question since 2026-10-19, when Daren declined an
open-source licence because Dice Roller might become a money-making idea.

A business launch post would need a sixth sentence (what people pay for),
and nothing in the repo or on the Desk says what that would be. So the post
written from today's evidence describes the current goal; a different one
would need a decision Daren hasn't made.

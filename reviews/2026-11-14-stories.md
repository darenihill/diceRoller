# User stories, walked through the app (2026-11-14)

Walked in Playwright (Chromium, 390×844, touch, 2× scale) on a `vite build`
of the release branch at f838790 (release 101: the bottom buttons drawn
apart). This pass walked the flows the 11 November walk didn't: typed rolls
and saved roll buttons, History reuse, Clear all, a share link on a fresh
phone, Table view, Hold, and Customize. Screenshots are in Daren's project
files under `dice-roller/2026-11-14/review54/`.

| # | Story | Test script | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Keep my sword roll as a button | RPG mode, type 2d6+3, Save this roll, name it Sword | Works: a "Sword 2d6+3" button under the box (`a-saved-roll.png`) |

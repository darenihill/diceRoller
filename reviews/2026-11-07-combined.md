# Combined (2026-11-07)

- **Backups, from two sides.** The walk lost three switches on a new phone
  (stories 6) and the security pass blanked the app with a damaged backup's
  stats (STRIDE, tampering). Same door: Import trusted or forgot parts of the
  file. Both are fixed, and the backup test now fails when a new saved value
  is left out, so the next switch can't be forgotten.
- **On or off, not by colour.** The Customize walk (story 8) and the SCAMPER
  Substitute both land on the same thing: on and off differ only by fill
  colour, which a colourblind player can't read. One change, switches that
  say On or Off, answers both. Kept for the Desk when it has room.
- **Saying what happens to your data.** The metrics switch didn't name Google
  Analytics (security), and nothing deleted can be got back after the Undo
  bar goes (stories, SCAMPER Adapt). Both are app standards: the wording is
  fixed now; Recently deleted is queued in GOAL.md.

# User stories, walked through the app, second pass (2026-11-09)

Walked in Playwright (Chromium, 390×844, touch, scale 1) against a production
build of the release branch after the trim pass (`npx vite build && npx vite
preview`). This pass looked for mis-taps and lost work: a double tap, a thumb
that lands a little off, a quick second action. Screenshots are in Daren's
project files under `dice-roller/2026-11-09/review45/`.

## Fixes built

- Story 4: the Undo bar ignores a press that starts within half a second of it
  showing, so a double tap on Remove, Delete or Clear all can't land on its ×
  or Undo. Keyboard and screen reader presses still work at once.
- Story 1: deleting a game while the bar is up joins the first delete ("Deleted
  2 games"); one Undo brings both back, each in its old place. Dice removed in a
  row join the same way ("Removed 2 dice").
- Story 2: Remove picture says "Removed picture" with Undo.

All three are tested in `src/test/review20261109b.test.tsx` and were walked
again in the browser after the build.

## Standards applied

- Undo | Main screen, die action row | Before: a double tap on Remove landed on the new Undo bar's × and threw the Undo away | After: the bar ignores a press in its first half second (`src/test/review20261109b.test.tsx`)
- Undo | Games, Your games | Before: a double tap on a bin deleted two games and Undo brought back one, at the end of the list | After: "Deleted 2 games", one Undo brings both back in their places (`src/test/review20261109b.test.tsx`)
- Undo | Die settings, Custom faces | Before: Remove picture had no message and no Undo | After: "Removed picture · Undo" puts the photo back (`src/test/review20261109b.test.tsx`)

# Goal check: jobs to be done (2026-10-22)

Question: what job does someone hire Dice Roller for, what do they use
instead today, and does the current goal aim at that job?

## The job

**"Our game needs dice we don't have in hand, set up the way this game wants,
right now, at the table."** Three variants show up in the evidence:

| Who | Moment | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Board-game table | Dice lost, or the game uses odd dice (Catan colours, LCR, Zombie Dice) | 12 presets; Catan and Classroom landing pages; "Most played games" in Usage Stats |
| RPG player | Needs a d20 with advantage and a modifier, fast | RPG mode, /dnd-dice-roller and /d20-roller pages; stories write-up task 6 |
| Teacher / maker | A die that doesn't exist: colours, words, pictures, symbols worth points | ROADMAP feedback: 3 asks for pictures (most of any request), Daniel's "M scores 6", Flip's "roll for a colour"; built 2026-10-12 to 10-17 |

The third row is what nothing else does well, and it is the word the goal
leads with: *customizable*.

## What they use instead

| Instead | Good at | Where Dice Roller wins |
|---|---|---|
| Physical dice | Feel, shared table | Custom faces, no lost dice, any count |
| Google's "roll a die" search box | Zero setup | Holds, presets, saves, custom faces |
| Phone dice apps (Play / App Store) | Shake to roll, polish | No install, no ads, share a set by link |
| Roll20 / D&D Beyond / Discord bots | Online RPG campaigns | No account; board games too |

Sources: Google Play dice roller search
(https://play.google.com/store/search?q=dice%20roller&c=apps); ROADMAP.md
"Discoverability" and Tier 1-3 (24 feedback responses).

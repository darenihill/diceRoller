# Slow spots (2026-10-26)

Measured on a production build in Chromium at 390 px with network held to
1.6 Mbps / 150 ms and the CPU slowed 4x (a mid-range phone on weak 4G).

| What | Measured | Feels |
|---|---|---|
| Open the app | First paint 0.94 s, ready 1.2 s. The app script is 135 KB over the wire; styles 6 KB. | Fine. (Opening on a hanging connection is already being built: "On a weak signal the app opens in 3 seconds".) |
| Roll with 20 dice | 90 frames in 1.5 s, average 18 ms a frame, worst 50 ms, none dropped past 50 ms. | Smooth. |
| Roll wait | The roll always takes 0.8 s. A second tap during it started a second roll: two History entries, and the first total briefly shown over the second roll's dice. | The one slow spot, and a bug. Fixed: a tap during the tumble is ignored. Focus idea: let that second tap land the dice at once instead. |
| Add 18 dice in a row | No lag between taps. | Fine. |
| Fonts | Roboto loads from Google Fonts (0.5 s on the slow network) with swap, so text shows at once in a fallback font. | Fine; self-hosting would save a lookup, not worth a change now. |

## Fix built

- A tap on Roll while the dice tumble no longer starts a second roll (unit
  test: two taps, one History entry).

No screen or action is slow apart from the deliberate roll animation.

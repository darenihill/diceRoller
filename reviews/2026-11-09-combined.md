# Combined (2026-11-09): stories, laws of UX, privacy

- **"Off" has to mean off, from three sides.** The stories walk turned metrics
  off and saw Google still contacted (story 3). Privacy found the same switch
  could be turned back on by a backup (story 4) and left Google's ID behind.
  Peak-end says the last moment of a choice is what people remember: a
  switch that says Off while Google still hears from the phone is the worst
  kind of end. All three are fixed in this pull request, and none of them
  changes anything on screen.
- **Deleting doesn't delete.** A removed photo stays on the phone (story 2),
  Clear History leaves Usage stats (story 5), and nothing wipes everything
  (privacy 5). The queued Recently deleted item is the same thread from the
  other end: it keeps things on purpose for 30 days. Building both together
  gives one clear rule: deleted things wait 30 days in Recently deleted, then
  are gone everywhere, pictures included.
- **The risky target is the easy one.** Fitts found the saved game's bin
  (48×48) is bigger than its name (62×34). The stories walk didn't trip on
  it only because Chrome on Android stretches a tap to the nearest button;
  an iPhone doesn't. Same shape as the face-row buttons already queued.
- **Where you land after a choice.** Keyboard and screen reader users lost
  their place after every menu dialog (story 11). Found only by walking it
  with keys; fixed.
